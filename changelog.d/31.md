section: Bug fixes
- [user] HEY Sync: a Todo whose title carries a literal zero-width space (U+200B) directly after an `@` — e.g. `Pay @<U+200B>rent` — now round-trips unchanged instead of reading back as `Pay @rent` and triggering a spurious Re-create that silently renamed the Todo in HEY. `encodeTitle` now escapes a pre-existing break sitting after an `@` so `decodeTitle` is a true, lossless inverse for every title (#31).
